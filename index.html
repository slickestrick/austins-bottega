He redesigned the end-to-end customer lifecycle, ran cohort analysis to build targeted campaigns, and supported a full sales org restructuring during a leadership transition.

During a CEO transition, Austin stepped in and ran Chainstack's entire commercial operation — sales, customer success, marketing, demand generation, developer relations, and partnerships. He owned the company sales goal outright.

2013 – 2017 · Exited 2025
Founder & Owner
St. Pete Beach LLC (SPB)
Built from a single Facebook page to a media business
Millions of monthly views · advertising, journalism, tourism
The city tried to shut it down. He sent them their own letter.

What started as a Facebook page for St. Pete Beach grew into a full media business. When Austin launched it, the city sent him a formal letter stating they didn't want the page and never would — so he kept it. For years it pulled millions of views per month.

SPB was spun out of Socialutions as the page outgrew its origins. The business expanded into advertising sales, journalism, and tourism promotion. Every new mayor tried to shut it down. Austin sent them the original letter. And carried on. He finally exited in 2025.
